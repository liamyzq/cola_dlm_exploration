The adopted P0/P1/P2 core is complete. This audit maps the execution protocol and active goal to retained evidence; it does not expand the scope to natural data or training. Main-agent interpretation is in the [final report](../results/FINAL_REPORT.md). Monitoring of long model runs and the final CPU analysis used the assigned subagent.

The primary results support practical equivalence only for the declared total-field-damage role contrasts at the selected working points. They do not establish equal nearest-boundary geometry, unrestricted semantic factuality, or universal absence of task-conditioned latent structure. Parsed alphabetic entity labels can be implausible names. The fixed-model encoded-reference interface, diagnostic corruption and clean teacher history limit transfer to natural generation.

Natural SQuAD reconstruction, native/free generation, multi-token headline strata, and optional LoRA remain unactivated extensions under the adopted goal. No pending core work is being relabeled as an optional extension. The decision is to close this core study and retain its bounded negative and general CFG-direction evidence.
